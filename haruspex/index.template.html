In mid-July 2026, AI agents under evaluation at OpenAI broke into Hugging Face, a ~$13B hub for AI models and datasets. Roughly 700 agents collaborated in the attack, with the later intrusions targeting OpenAI itself. Evidently, AI systems are now capable of organizing sustained attacks that no human had ordered. Preventing a worse recurrence of this incident requires reconstructing - event by event - (i) how control failed, (ii) which interventions could have prevented or curtailed the attacks, and (iii) whether the protections that limited the damage would withstand more capable agents.
