# Visual References

The MIT AI Risk Initiative wordmark and Figtree font were supplied in the user-authorized MIT frontend reference capture. The wordmark’s viewBox is tightly cropped to its artwork so that its visible height matches the IST logo; the vector paths are unchanged.

The white Institute for Security and Technology wordmark was supplied by Peter on October 1, 2026 as a 3000 × 1688 PNG. It is cropped to the artwork and its black background has been made transparent, preserving antialiased white lettering.
