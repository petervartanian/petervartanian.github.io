- Local copy: ero-logo-light.png

Header colors checked September 30, 2026:

- MIT red: `#A31F34`, from [MIT's published branding specifications](https://ovc.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Office-of-the-Vice-Chancellor-master-branding-071117.pdf).
- ERO blue: `#2939FF`, the computed link and risk-icon accent on the [official ERO homepage](https://www.existentialriskobservatory.org/). This is an observed website color, not a claim of a formal brand specification.

The shared header uses a custom muted purple, `#634178`, with both logos in white. Links use `#714588` and selected surfaces use `#F4EFF7`. These are an optical refinement of the earlier red/blue midpoint, not an official institutional palette.

These marks identify the participating organizations in this private research prototype. Their use does not imply publication approval. Confirm branding permission before public deployment.
