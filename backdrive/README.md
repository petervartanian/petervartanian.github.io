# Catalog of 1+2+3

Public incident, capability, and evaluation records, with proposed interventions and pilots. Back\Drive focuses the catalog on robotics.

The Sage Plant is the featured pilot proposal, with Dusty FieldPrinter 2 and Derutu DM Leading + PC-16 cases. Plans, quotes, and reported baselines are attributed to the September 28, 2026 discussion draft. No field results are recorded and MIT participation and scope remain unconfirmed.

Equipment-specific manufacturer guidance is used where available. Incident proxies compare particular hazards on other machines and do not establish faults in the pilot equipment. Published methods, manufacturer guidance, and proposed comparisons are labeled separately. The case notes identify conflicting PC16 specifications and the unresolved baseline calculation.
