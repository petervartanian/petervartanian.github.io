Contact icons are stored locally so the page does not depend on an external icon service.

- LinkedIn, GitHub, envelope, and telephone: Bootstrap Icons v1.13.1, MIT license (see LICENSE-bootstrap.txt). https://github.com/twbs/icons/tree/v1.13.1
- Substack, X, Google Scholar, and ORCID: Simple Icons v16.0.0, CC0. https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.0.0
- Hugging Face: official brand artwork. https://huggingface.co/front/assets/huggingface_logo-noborder.svg

Monochrome marks use their brand colors; email and phone use the contact page accent. Marks identify the linked services; ownership remains with their respective owners.
